ashtadhyayi.com
Neelesh Bodas

The comprehensive study portal for Pāṇinian grammar, built and maintained by Neelesh Bodas since 2015. The Studio's grammar data comes from the site's public data repository (ashtadhyayi-com/data, commit a9f9112, the version the site serves):

  • the Aṣṭādhyāyī sūtrapāṭha with padaccheda, anuvṛtti, sūtrārtha, Kāśikā, Siddhānta Kaumudī, Laghu Kaumudī, Bālamanoramā and vārttikas
  • the Dhātupāṭha and its conjugation and kṛdanta tables; the Śabdapāṭha with its declensions and prakriyā
  • Śiva-sūtras, pratyāhāras, gaṇapāṭha, uṇādi-sūtras, liṅgānuśāsana and Pāṇinīya Śikṣā
  • seven kośas: Bhāratī, Abhyankar's grammar dictionary, Amarakośa, Halāyudha, Vaijayantī, Ākhyātacandrikā and Upasargārthacandrikā

Every "ashtadhyayi.com ↗" button opens the same sūtra, dhātu or śabda on ashtadhyayi.com, which has much more: all its commentaries, Mahābhāṣya, Nyāsa, Padamañjarī, prakriyā, audio and courses. Corrections to this data belong on ashtadhyayi.com (use its feedback form).

Andhra Bharati · ఆంధ్రభారతి
andhrabharati.com

Andhra Bharati's Sanskrit dictionary page brings twelve classical dictionaries together in one search, with transliteration keyboards and display in Telugu and other Indian scripts. The Studio offers the same twelve dictionaries, and its character map follows the spirit of Andhra Bharati's Font Help.

Nothing from andhrabharati.com is copied here: its pages, code and Telugu resources stay on its own site, and the "Andhra Bharati ↗" buttons take you there. The dictionary texts themselves come from the Cologne Digital Sanskrit Lexicon (below), the same texts Andhra Bharati displays.
